sections:
  - name: Project Goal
    content: >-
      Create a brand new budget mobile app to help people create and stick to a budget. The app will
      put all aspects of their budgets just a few taps away.
  - name: Target Audience
    content: >-
      The primary audience is young adults and young professionals who have never used a budget
      before. The secondary audience is young adults who have tried budget apps but found them too
      complex.

  - name: Research & Key Challenges
    content: >-
      I used Google Docs questionnaires and remote interviews for a wide range of ages. The key
      challenges were to limit the features to just those which will educate first-time budgeters
      about budget concepts. The participants said that budget apps they tried were too complex so I
      kept the feature set limited.

  - name: Initial Design Concepts & Wireframes
    content: >-
      First I drew many hand sketches to capture my initial design ideas using the Crazy 8s method.
      The best ideas from my initial design concepts were translated into digital wireframes in
      Figma.
    images:
      - url: /images/product-design/budget-app/initial-design-concepts-and-wireframes-1.jpg

  - name: Usability Testing
    content: >-
      Participants reviewed the digital wireframes and answered questions about navigation and
      budget concepts. Their feedback was recorded in a spreadsheet for each participant. The
      responses were transformed into affinity groups to find themes.
    images:
      - url: /images/product-design/budget-app/usability-testing-1.jpg
        alt: Usability issues and insights gathered from tester one during wireframe prototype use.
      - url: /images/product-design/budget-app/usability-testing-2.jpg
        alt: Usability issues and insights gathered from tester two during wireframe prototype use.
  - name: Conclusion
    content: >-
      The budget app design is neutral. I decided not to make it fun or serious because the apps
      tried by my participants fell into one or the other style and it didn't help them. The app has
      light and dark modes which are warm and approachable.
